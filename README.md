# Bank of America Investment Banking Forage Job Simulation
Investment Banking Job Simulation by Bank of America through Forage!

## Overview
The **Bank of America Investment Banking Job Simulation** provided hands-on experience with real-world business problems, Throughout this job simulation, I learned what it takes to be an investment banker at Bank of America. I familiarize myself with the elements we do in our investment banking practice including communication, M&A screening, relationship building, and financial modeling. By completing the job simulation, I have built the skills to set me up for success in my career in investment banking at Bank of America.

---

## Task 1: Understanding the Company: Historical Financial Performance & SWOT Analysis
Review the client’s Form-10K (annual report) to understand its historical financial performance and the internal and external factors (SWOT analysis) that support its strategic positioning.
- Identify threats for a SWOT analysis of a subscription streaming service
- Create a financial summary in Excel

---

## Task 2: Strategic Alternatives Available to the Company
Review the conference transcript to determine the best M&A target and method of raising capital.
- Identify the best potential target for Webflix based on an assessment of their strategic criteria.

---

## Task 3: Financial Modeling
Create a simple build-up of a financial model of the target to understand potential future cash flows.
- Calculate the implied equity value and share value of Nintendo using the DCF valuation method
- Construct a sensitivity table showing how the valuation of Nintendo will change if variables change
- Write an email to your supervisors Susan and Paula summarizing your findings

---

## Task 4: Communicating Successfully
Create a slide deck and write an email to communicate and present recommendations.
- Create charts in Excel showing the acquisition target’s EBITDA each year and revenue by geography
- Create a company profile for Nintendo summarizing key information about the company
- Write an email to your supervisors attaching your company profile

---

## Task 5: The Art of Building Long-Term Relationships
Effectively building and maintaining relationships with clients for growth opportunities and referrals.
- Leave a voicemail for a Nintendo executive requesting a meeting

---

## Key Learnings
Through this virtual internship, I gained valuable experience in:
- Why a SWOT analysis is useful for understanding the competitive position of a business
- How to use a 10-K to prepare a financial summary
- How to select an acquisition target aligned with your client’s needs
- How a discounted cashflow model (DCF) is used to value an acquisition target
- What a sensitivity analysis is
- What information is contained in a company profile and how it is used by investment bankers
- Why building and maintaining relationships is vitally important in the banking industry
